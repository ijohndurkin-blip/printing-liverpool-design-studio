    Frequently asked questions

    What file should I upload?

    A transparent PNG at 300 dpi, or a vector file (SVG, AI, PDF), gives the sharpest print. The studio warns you if your image looks too low-resolution for the size you've chosen.

    Is there a minimum order?

    Not for DTG, vinyl or embroidery. You can order just one shirt. Screen printing starts at 20 items because of the set-up cost.

    I don't have artwork. Can you help?

    Yes. go cre8 has in-house graphic designers. Tick Get help with your artwork and we'll tidy up your logo or design something from your brief.

    What can you print on?

    T-shirts, polos, hoodies, sweatshirts, sportswear, workwear, safety wear, bags and accessories. We can also make the matching banners, signage and exhibition graphics for your event.

    How fast can you turn it around?

    Standard orders take 5–7 working days after you approve the proof. Express and Rush options are available if you need them sooner.

    Can I add names or numbers to each shirt?

    Yes. Add a note on the order form and we'll send you a simple spreadsheet to fill in.
